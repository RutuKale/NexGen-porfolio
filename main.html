FAQ

Answers to common questions

How long does custom software development take? +
Timelines depend heavily on complexity. A basic app might take 2–4 months, while complex enterprise systems can take 6–12 months or longer.
What information do you need to start a project? +
We need a clear outline of goals, target audience, functional requirements, and any existing brand guidelines or technical infrastructure.
How do I start a digital marketing campaign? +
It involves defining goals, identifying the target audience, selecting platforms (SEO, PPC, Social), creating content, and setting a budget.
How long does it take to see SEO results? +
SEO is a long-term strategy; significant results typically appear in 3–6 months, though this varies based on competition.
What services does your full-service agency offer? +
We provide a comprehensive range of services, including SEO, content creation, social media management, website design, and marketing automation.
How do you ensure product quality? +
We employ rigorous QA (Quality Assurance) testing, including functional, usability, and performance testing before deployment.
